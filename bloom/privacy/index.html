Bloom Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 November 2026

Bloom keeps your data on your device. We do not collect, share or sell it.

About this policy

This policy applies to the Bloom mobile app (package name com.rishabhtyagi.bloom), published by Rishabh Tyagi ("we", "us").

Information we collect

None. Bloom has no accounts, ads, analytics, tracking or crash reporting. The app does not send your information to us or to anyone else.

Information stored on your device

To work, Bloom stores the following in its private storage on your device. We cannot access it.

  • The name you enter
  • Your chosen avatar or cropped photo
  • Your habits and their settings
  • Your daily check-ins
  • Your theme choice

Photos

Adding a photo is optional. If you choose to, Bloom opens your device's photo picker and uses only the photo you select. It saves a small cropped copy without location or camera details. Your original photo is not changed.

Sharing

We do not share or sell any information, because we do not receive any.

Security and backups

Bloom's data is kept in the app's private storage, protected by your device's operating system. Bloom asks the system to leave its data out of device backups. If you lose your device or uninstall Bloom, we cannot recover your data.

Keeping and deleting your data

Your data stays on your device until you delete it. You can:

  • Delete a habit, which also removes its check-ins
  • Open Settings and choose Reset app to remove everything
  • Uninstall Bloom

Children

We do not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

App stores and this page

The store you download Bloom from may process information under its own privacy policy. This page is hosted on GitHub Pages, and GitHub may log technical information about visits, such as IP addresses, under the GitHub Privacy Statement.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will post the new version here and update the date above.
